Entry for make.fun's "Weirdest Job Title: Blind Judge Showdown" bounty: one job title, 30 characters, the weirdest we could think of — **Office Chair Gas Lift Eulogist**. Everybody has watched an office chair slowly sink to its lowest setting and sighed; this job gives that moment a funeral, with a eulogist delivering a short tribute every time a chair's gas lift gives up. A real-sounding job (eulogist) with one hilariously specific twist (the patient is a chair piston), instantly readable at a glance.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the title, with no trailing newline, to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`). Pure Python standard library, `os` import only.
